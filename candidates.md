# Candidates — Dad Joke Showdown

Thirteen candidate jokes were drafted, counted exactly by script, and duelled pairwise on the judge's criteria in order, each read aloud deadpan; the winner then defended its place against three fresh challengers in a revision round.

**Final winner (155 chars):** Your mother says I'm obsessed with degrees. She's right — I have one from college, and one on the thermostat that nobody in this house is allowed to touch.

**Runner-up (151 chars):** At your sister's graduation dinner, I stood up to make a toast. Your mother whispered, "Finally — a toast of yours that won't set off the smoke alarm."
